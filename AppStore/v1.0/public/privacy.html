MAC UART 隐私政策

适用于 macOS 版 MAC UART 1.0。

设备通信与本机存储

本应用无需账户，不包含广告或分析 SDK。开发者不会通过应用收集收发内容、设备地址或使用记录。发送串口数据时，内容会传给连接的设备；使用 TCP 时，内容会传给你指定或选中的对端。TCP 采用明文传输。对端可按其自身设置记录收到的数据。

保存的快捷指令、串口参数和语言偏好存放在你的 Mac 上。屏幕中的通信记录保存在内存中。选择导出或录制后，应用会在你选定的位置写入包含时间、方向、设备路径或对端地址及消息内容的文件。若选择云同步目录，文件可能由相应的同步服务传输。

管理与联系

你可以在应用中删除快捷指令、清空屏幕记录，并在访达中删除保存的日志。清空屏幕不会删除已经写入磁盘的文件。如果你主动发送支持邮件，开发者会收到你的邮箱、邮件正文和你选择附上的文件，用于处理该请求。你可以联系 xiangw@itron.com.cn 申请删除你提交的支持资料。有关应用隐私的疑问，请通过支持页面联系开发者。

MAC UART Privacy Policy

Applies to MAC UART 1.0 for macOS.

Device communication and local storage

The app requires no account and includes no advertising or analytics SDKs. The developer does not collect message contents, device addresses or usage history through the app. Serial data is sent to the connected device. TCP data is sent in plain text to the peer you specify or select. The peer may retain data according to its own settings.

Saved commands, serial settings and language preferences stay on your Mac. The on-screen traffic log is held in memory. When you export or record, the app writes timestamps, directions, device paths or peer addresses, and message contents to the location you choose. If you choose a cloud-synchronised folder, your sync service may transfer those files.

Controls and contact

You can delete saved commands and clear the on-screen log in the app. You can remove saved log files in Finder. Clearing the screen leaves saved files in place. If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, to handle your request. You can request deletion of the support materials you supplied by emailing xiangw@itron.com.cn. For privacy questions, contact the developer via the support page.
